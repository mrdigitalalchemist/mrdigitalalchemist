## Activated Cloud

**AI models are just engines. Activated Cloud is the vehicle.**

Agents can finally do real work — but they have nowhere to live. A laptop dies
when the lid closes; a cloud VM gets blocked by the sites that matter; and setup
loses everyone at the terminal. So I'm building the computer for them.

**Activated Cloud OS** gives an agent what a worker needs — memory, real tools, a
schedule, and boundaries you control. It installs like a consumer app: one
download, the entire runtime inside, a two-minute setup wizard, and a working
agent. No terminal, no account.

## How I build

<details>
<summary><b>Local-first, by design</b></summary>

<br/>

The agent, its memory, and your model keys live on your machine — not on my
servers. It's a smaller breach surface, a cleaner privacy story, and the reason
people trust an agent with their email, files, and money. Local-first isn't
ideology here; it's the unlock.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Consumer-grade install</b></summary>

<br/>

The whole runtime ships inside the app — a relocatable interpreter with every
dependency bundled. That's the hard part nobody else did: a two-minute,
zero-terminal setup a non-technical person can complete. It's the adoption gate,
and it's solved.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Boundaries you can see</b></summary>

<br/>

Every skill is a switch; what's off cannot run. Actions that need your consent
reach your phone. Trust is a setting, not a promise.

</details>
